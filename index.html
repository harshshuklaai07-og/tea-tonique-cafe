Our Story

Tea Tonique Cafe is more than just a cafe — it's a destination for tea and coffee lovers seeking quality, comfort, and community.

Located in the vibrant city of Nanded, Maharashtra, we pride ourselves on serving premium beverages and delicious food in a warm, welcoming atmosphere. Whether you're catching up with friends, working on your laptop, or simply enjoying a quiet moment, Tea Tonique is your perfect escape.

Our carefully curated menu features an extensive selection of teas from around the world, expertly crafted coffees, refreshing beverages, and mouth-watering snacks and meals — all made with love.
