Our Story

Tea Tonique is Nagpur's beloved chai katta — a place where great tea meets great company.

Located opposite Yashwant Stadium in Dhantoli, we've been serving Nagpur's tea lovers with kadak masala chai, freshly made snacks, and a warm, welcoming vibe. Our tagline says it all — Chaha Ukltoy (tea is boiling).

Whether you're dropping in for a quick cutting chai or settling in for a full meal, Tea Tonique is your everyday escape from the rush of the city.
